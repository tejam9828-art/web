Bayerische Motoren Werke AG, commonly known as BMW, is a famous German company that makes luxury cars and motorcycles. Founded in 1916, the company started by building aircraft engines before moving on to motorcycles in the 1920s and cars in the 1928. Headquartered in Munich, Germany, BMW also owns other well-known brands like MINI and Rolls-Royce. Today, the brand is known around the world for making sporty, high-performance vehicles and is working hard on new electric and sustainable car technology. Since you are open to it, let's look at a few of BMW's most famous and popular car models that helped build its reputation as the "Ultimate Driving Machine": BMW 3 Series: The brand's most famous compact executive car, celebrated worldwide for its perfect balance of everyday comfort and sharp, sporty handling. BMW 5 Series: A mid-size luxury sedan that offers more space and high-end tech, making it a favorite for business professionals who still love to drive. BMW M3 / M5: The high-performance versions developed by BMW's motorsport division, known for track-ready power and aggressive styling. BMW X5: One of the pioneers of the luxury SUV market, combining all-wheel-drive utility with the driving dynamics of a sports sedan. BMW i4 / iX: Part of the modern electric vehicle (EV) lineup, showing the brand's shift toward sustainable power without sacrificing performance.
